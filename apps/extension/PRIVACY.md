# Privacy Policy for Navidrome Web Player

**Last Updated:** 27.11.2025

## 1. Overview
Navidrome Web Player ("the Extension") is a browser extension designed to interface with a user's self-hosted Navidrome server. The Extension operates as a client-side application.

## 2. Data Collection and Usage
The Developer ("we", "us") does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data from users of the Extension.

*   **No Analytics:** We do not track your usage or install any third-party analytics scripts.
*   **No Remote Server:** We do not operate a backend server for this Extension. The Extension communicates exclusively between your browser and your personal Navidrome server.

## 3. Local Data Storage
To function correctly, the Extension stores specific data locally on your device using the browser's storage APIs:

*   **Server URL:** The address of your self-hosted Navidrome instance.
*   **Authentication Tokens:** Used to maintain your login session with your server.
*   **Preferences:** UI settings such as theme, volume, and last played song.
*   **Cache:** Album artwork and music metadata may be temporarily cached to improve performance.

This data never leaves your browser and is not accessible to the Developer or any third parties.

## 4. Permissions
*   **Host Permissions:** The Extension requests permission to access URLs provided by the user. This is strictly required to connect to your specific self-hosted server domain or IP address to stream audio.
*   **Storage:** Required to save your login state and preferences.
